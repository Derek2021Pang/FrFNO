# Section 5.6 / Appendix — Theory verification

Top level:
* `theory_verify.py` — Exp.1 (grid-increment floor) and Exp.2 (s-scaling of
  FrFNO vs FNO); writes `theory_verify_result.txt`.

`theory_closure/` — Exp.3–5 (uses the legacy `frfno_legacy.py` at the package
root; run order is encoded in run_all.py):
* `theory_p15_p14.py` — Exp.3/4 measured slopes in T, eta, beta and the Born
  spectral ratio (`p15_p14_*.txt`).
* `p0_platform.py`, `both_train.py`, `p0_both_eval.py`, `kscan_train.py`,
  `trscan_train.py`, `p0_trscan.py`, `probe_spec.py` — Exp.5 error-floor and
  two-bandwidth study (K-scan and training-resolution scan); summaries in
  `p0eval.out`, `p0treval.out`, `exp5_both_summary.md`.

Large trained weights (`*.pt`) and the 49 MB reference field are regenerated by
these scripts and are not committed; small text/npz/png artifacts are included.
